Questions from the floor

Is this the digital euro?

No. It is a cat. The digital euro is a project of the European Central Bank. This is a meme coin on Solana that pays holders in EURC. Similar energy, different legal status.

Why a British Shorthair?

Because it is the only cat breed that looks like it has read the Maastricht Treaty. We are aware of the irony of the word "British". So is the cat.

Where do the euros come from?

From trading volume. StonkFun collects the 3% transfer tax on $EUROCAT and pays it to holders in EURC. High volume, more euros. No volume, no euros. That's the whole balance sheet.

Is there a roadmap?

There is a windowsill. The Governing Council of One will reassess its position when the sun moves.

What is the collateral?

Vibes, denominated in euros. $EUROCAT itself has no intrinsic value and no claim on anything. EURC is issued by Circle and is not affiliated with this token.

Whatever it takes?

Within our mandate. And believe us, it will be enough.
